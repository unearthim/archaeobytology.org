Phase 1: The Triage Classifications

Every excavated Archaeobyte is classified by its functional state. This Triage determines its value as a Proof, a Warning, or a Blueprint.

🌱

The Vivibyte (Living)

Function: Intact

Metaphor: "Gold Coin" or "Seed Bank"

A file whose native function is still legible and useful today. Examples include `.mp3`, `.gif`, and open `HTML 4.01`.

Lesson: The Proof of Resilience

Openness and backward compatibility are the most enduring traits.

🔗

The Umbrabyte (Liminal)

Function: Living File, Dead Context

Metaphor: "Fly in Amber"

A file that is preserved, but its surrounding ecosystem (links, community) is petrified. Examples: GeoCities pages, MySpace Top 8.

Lesson: The Warning Against Rented Land

Building on centralized platforms leads to inevitable contextual collapse.

🦕

The Petribyte (Petrified)

Function: Extinct

Metaphor: "Fossil of Function"

A concept or file whose original purpose is now obsolete. Examples: The AIM Away Message, the `guestbook.cgi` script.

Lesson: The Blueprint for a Wiser Web

Fossils of function reveal lost human-centric design principles.

The Goal: The Anvil

The Anvil is the applied philosophy of Archaeobytology: using the wisdom (Proof, Warning, Blueprint) from the Archive to forge resilient, human-centric future frameworks.

📣

Pillar 1: Declaration

Building tools that prioritize user self-definition and intentional presence (the lesson of the "Away Message").

🤝

Pillar 2: Connection

Forging hand-built, non-algorithmic bridges between users (the lesson of the "Webring").

🏛️

Pillar 3: The Ground

Designing systems that guarantee user sovereignty over their data and property (the lesson of "GeoCities").
