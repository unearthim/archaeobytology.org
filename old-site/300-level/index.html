ARCH 300

Advanced Archaeobytology

Capstone Seminar: From Theory to Practice

A hands-on capstone course where students apply archaeobytological principles to real-world preservation challenges and sovereign system design. Build, preserve, and forge your contribution to digital sovereignty.

Level
Advanced / 300
Format
10 Modules
Credits
4 Units
Type
Project-Based
← Textbook Institute Home

Prerequisites

  • Completion of ARCH 200 (or equivalent foundational knowledge in Archaeobytology)
  • Familiarity with the Three Pillars framework (Declaration, Connection, Ground)
  • Basic understanding of digital preservation methods and triage methodology
  • Ability to work independently on a semester-long capstone project
